Privacy

Effective date: October 12, 2026.

Codex Usage for Android is an unofficial, free, open-source app published by getClear Digital and maintained in the project repository. It has no advertising or developer-operated server. The app explicitly disables the bundled app-server's analytics and telemetry exporters.

Sign-in and network requests

The app runs the official Codex app-server on your device. Signing in opens OpenAI's sign-in page in your browser. Authentication and usage requests go to OpenAI over encrypted connections. Your account is governed by OpenAI's privacy policy. This app does not create a separate account or send prompts or conversations. OpenAI may process account identifiers, authentication information, connection metadata, and usage requests when providing its service.

Information stored on your device

The app-server stores your sign-in credentials in Android's private app storage. The app stores a separate display cache containing usage percentages, limit durations, reset times, available reset counts and expiries, and the time of the last successful update. A local diagnostic file records restricted status flags and displayable quota information, without raw authentication/account responses or server error output. Preferences store refresh status and scheduling times. Android backups are disabled. Other apps cannot normally access this private storage. Anyone who can see your home-screen widget can see the quota it displays.

Retention and deletion

Successful updates replace the display cache. Failed requests retain the last successful values. Sign out removes the saved sign-in and display cache; it does not delete your OpenAI account or revoke every session on other devices. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its remaining local files and preferences. Manage your OpenAI account and data directly with OpenAI.

Permissions and background activity

Internet and network-state access support sign-in and quota reads. A temporary foreground service supports a refresh or sign-in in progress. Android schedules background refresh while a widget is installed; device power policies may delay it. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or shared storage access.

Contact and changes

For support or privacy questions, email codex-usage@getclear.digital. For public bug reports and project questions, use the project's issue tracker. Issues are public: never include credentials, account responses, or personal information. For a private security report, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature if enabled. Policy changes will be recorded in this repository.
